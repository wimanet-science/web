## WiMa-Games
A team composed of several participants from the WG2 of WIMANET has been developing small games to practice the vocabulary, notions and your skills in relation to wildlife malaria and related blood parasites morphological characters and identification. <br> <br>
Want to practice in a fun and quick way? Try our creative games! <br> <br>
